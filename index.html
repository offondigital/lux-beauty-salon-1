Our Approach

Luxury Without
the Waste.

LUX is a boutique, sustainability-minded studio. Small team, deliberate pace, and product lines chosen for what they leave out as much as what they put in.

  • Wella Professional color, mixed per guest to limit excess
  • Arete color and home care for low-impact maintenance
  • Innersense Organic Beauty professional hair care
  • Appointment-only scheduling to reduce water and energy use
